# Crypto market judgment

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Current venue/account packet and stream health.
2. Current raw-derived market descriptions and calibrated model outputs.
3. Recent attributable decisions, receipts, trades, and outcomes.
4. Evidence-linked active guidance and memory.
5. Inference.

## Comparative record

For long, short, and no trade, record:

- current auction and acceptance;
- setup phase and maturity;
- entry now versus wait;
- genuine invalidation;
- plausible objective and remaining room;
- expected fill and adverse selection;
- target-before-stop evidence;
- fees, spread, slippage, funding, and latency;
- strongest disconfirming evidence;
- conservative net expectancy.

Use microprice, order-flow imbalance, trade aggression, absorption, replenishment, depletion, volatility, local range location, cross-venue dispersion, mark/index divergence, funding, and open interest as relational evidence. None is a standalone signal.

A profitable dollar amount created only by leverage is not edge. Reject opportunities whose objective is ordinary noise or whose gross path is consumed by friction.
