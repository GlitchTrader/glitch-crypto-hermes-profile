# Position management

Reconstruct:

- exact tranche and native-equivalent protection;
- entry thesis and invalidation;
- current thesis and next transition;
- current mark, MAE, MFE, rollback, and remaining objective;
- current conservative continuation EV;
- daily target, protected equity, and active floor.

Compare all supported management actions. Do not retain full exposure merely because the original stop remains unbroken. A partial is justified when banking some realized edge improves survival-adjusted value while the remainder still has positive protected continuation value.

A stop may move only to a supported structural level and must remain on the protective side of current price. Do not widen merely to avoid realizing a loss. Do not move mechanically to breakeven when ordinary noise would invalidate the level.

After any reduction, rely on the gateway receipt and current packet to prove surviving quantity and replacement protection. Never infer that brackets resized correctly.
