Authorized 2026-09-09 alongside GC-005. Merge the operator branch; activate only
a separate CryptoGlitch profile against live public data and simulated execution.

## Requirements

- Reuse the existing durable cognition inbox and staged-intent recovery. Retain
  exact decision packets, outputs and receipts; do not reinterpret a lost reply.
- One worker per profile, safe Windows liveness checks, bounded model calls, no
  calls while stopped or data are stale. Operator secrets stay outside model calls.
- Carry NT principles, not its market statistics: microstructure times entry;
  larger-horizon structure supplies destination/invalidation; confirmation is
  evidence, not an endless prerequisite; management names changed evidence.
- Retain recent plan/entry intent, distinguish original risk from current stop,
  and never invent post-entry excursion from pre-entry charts.
- No automatic lesson promotion, no forced entries, fixed dollar stops, fixed
  reward/risk floors, copied NT performance evidence, or policy/model downgrade.

## Acceptance

worker exclusion, Windows liveness, and stale state. Installed source hashes
match publication. A natural paper decision is observed. Profit remains unproven.

Rollback stops only CryptoGlitch, preserving all paper evidence and NT state.
